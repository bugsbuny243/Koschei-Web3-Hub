Every conclusion keeps its boundary.

Premium security intelligence is not a prettier risk score. It is a better chain of custody for what the system actually knows.

01

Observed is not verified

An RPC response can establish observed state without silently becoming independent verification.

02

Permission is not safety

An allowance, delegation or upgrade authority is a control surface—not an automatic malicious verdict.

03

Absence is not proof

No standard proxy slot observed does not mean a contract is immutable or free of another upgrade mechanism.
