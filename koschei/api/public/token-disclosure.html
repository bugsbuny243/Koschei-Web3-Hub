RISK DISCLOSURE · PUBLIC INFORMATION

A token is not a software entitlement, safety certificate, or investment guarantee.

Token prices and liquidity can change sharply. Public token activity does not prove product adoption, customer revenue, security quality, identity, intent, or future value. Koschei Web3 evidence outputs must be evaluated on their own signed/evidence contracts.

Commercial separation

KOSCH holdings, wallet balances and historical token tiers do not authorize Koschei Web3 operational access. Professional access is determined server-side.

No safety inference

Absence of a known issue is not proof of safety. Missing or unavailable evidence remains unknown rather than being converted into a positive claim.

No access inference

Owning, transferring, or selling a related token does not create a customer account, API entitlement, quota, or support commitment.

Verify public records

Use independently verifiable on-chain records and the treasury disclosure where relevant. Do not treat marketing or social-media claims as a substitute for evidence.
