First-response policy and limits

Critical: 15 min · High: 1 hour · Medium: 4 hours · Low: 24 hours · Info: 48 hours. These are internal targets, not a contractual guarantee.

The clock starts at incident creation and uses the severity in the original creation event. The earliest explicit acknowledgement or investigation, containment, resolution or close event counts as a response. Reopening does not reset the first-response clock. Missing creation history or inconsistent timestamps remain UNKNOWN.

The queue polls every 15 seconds while this page is visible. The selected case refreshes on request so your unsaved inputs stay intact. This is a shared case workspace, not a chat or external war-room integration.
