# Koschei Web3 Hub — standalone product charter v1

Koschei Web3 Hub is an independent commercial product. It is not a bundle that contains Koschei Sentinel or Koschei Lang, and it is sold separately from both.

## Product mission

Build a world-class crypto security and intelligence platform that continuously investigates on-chain and public ecosystem evidence, links related activity, detects suspicious patterns and presents defensible findings to customers.

The product must not reduce itself to a token scanner. Its target scope is the full crypto attack and manipulation surface that can be evidenced safely and lawfully.

## Core intelligence scope

Web3 Hub / ARVIS should investigate and correlate, where evidence is available:

- token and smart-contract risk, authority and upgrade/control surfaces;
- malicious or deceptive contract behavior and suspicious transaction patterns;
- liquidity creation, removal, migration and manipulation patterns;
- coordinated trading, wash-like behavior, artificial volume and market-manipulation indicators;
- creator/deployer history and repeat-operator behavior;
- multi-wallet and sybil-style wallet clusters;
- funding relationships, transfer graphs and recurring wallet infrastructure;
- sniper, bundled and early-entry timing patterns;
- cross-token and cross-project actor reuse;
- coordinated groups and communities when there is attributable public or on-chain evidence;
- promotion/hype campaigns and public claims, with provenance and confidence labels;
- phishing, fake claim surfaces, fake contracts and impersonation indicators;
- MEV and transaction-execution risk;
- suspicious treasury, mint, freeze, ownership and privileged-control behavior;
- bridge/cross-chain movement and multi-network relationships where supported;
- historical incident memory and repeat patterns across launches and ecosystems.

## Evidence rule

No evidence, no claim.

The system may identify correlation, coordination or suspicious patterns, but must distinguish verified facts, inferred relationships and unknown/missing evidence. It must not present speculation as identity attribution or criminal fact.

## ARVIS role

ARVIS is the investigation, evidence-correlation, scanning and decision engine inside Koschei Web3 Hub. It collects and normalizes evidence, builds relationships, applies deterministic rules where appropriate, preserves history and produces customer-facing findings.

## Customer delivery

Customers should be able to receive findings through the Web3 product surfaces, reports, API/webhooks and opted-in notification channels such as the existing Telegram bot. Telegram belongs to Web3 customer delivery; it is not a reason to merge Sentinel or Lang into Web3.

## Independent-product boundary

Koschei Sentinel and Koschei Lang are separate products with separate commercial plans and roadmaps.

Optional interoperability is allowed only through explicit contracts/adapters. Buying Web3 does not automatically buy Sentinel or Lang. Web3 must remain usable without either product.

## Commercial rule

Koschei Web3 Hub has its own pricing, entitlement, billing and customer-access policy. Do not advertise Sentinel or Lang as included features of a Web3 plan unless a future explicit commercial decision changes that policy.
